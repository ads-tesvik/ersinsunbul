Independent Researcher · Ankara

Dr. Ersin Sünbül

International trade, financial econometrics and logistics

Email: ersinsunbul [at] hotmail.com · ORCID: 0000-0001-6187-2038 · ads-tesvik.github.io/ersinsunbul/en

Dr. Ersin Sünbül is a researcher who applies time-series econometrics and machine-learning forecasting methods to problems in international trade, finance and logistics. The main research topics are exchange rates, inflation and monetary policy, crypto-assets, tourism receipts, card payments and trade finance. Most of the studies compare classical econometric models with artificial neural networks on the same data, testing which method yields more accurate forecasts under which conditions.

Dr. Sünbül completed a Ph.D. in International Trade at Ankara Hacı Bayram Veli University in 2022. A second doctoral thesis, in Business Administration at Kırıkkale University, develops a scale that measures leaders’ power distance preferences in the context of Turkish culture. From 2023 to 2025, Dr. Sünbül taught artificial intelligence, international finance and logistics as an Assistant Professor at Istanbul Nişantaşı University, and is the author of the books Uluslararası Ticaretin Finansmanı [International Trade Finance] (2026) and Proje Lojistiği [Project Logistics] (2024). Currently based in Ankara as an independent researcher, Dr. Sünbül also contributes to project development for national and international funding programmes.

Research interests

  • Time-series econometrics

    Linear and nonlinear models: ARDL, VAR/VECM, NARDL, Toda–Yamamoto causality, quantile regression and panel methods.

  • Forecasting with artificial intelligence

    Artificial neural networks, LSTM, GRU and RNN; hybrid ARIMA–ANN and MIDAS–LSTM models; natural language processing.

  • Money, finance and markets

    Exchange rates, inflation and monetary policy, crypto-assets, card payments and financial inclusion.

  • International trade and trade finance

    Foreign trade, the trade finance gap, global value chains and tourism receipts.

  • Logistics and supply chains

    Disaster logistics and location–allocation optimisation, green supply chains, project logistics.

  • Management and leadership

    Strategic management and planning; personality, leadership and power distance in cultural context.
